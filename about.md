I'm Sri, an AI Research Lead working on **real-time digital humans and
generative AI**: photorealistic avatars that you can talk to, built on 3D
Gaussian Splatting, diffusion models and a lot of inference optimization.

I lead the research team at [Beyond Presence](https://www.beyondpresence.ai).
Before that I led performance capture and animation research at Colossyan,
built multi-player motion capture for sports broadcasting at Sporttotal, and
was an early engineer at Meshcapade (now Epic Games), working on SMPL
digital humans. I studied Computer Science at
[TU Munich](https://www.tum.de), focusing on vision and graphics.

When I'm away from the screen, I'm usually behind a camera: film and digital,
at home in Munich and on the road. Some of it ends up [here]({{ '/photography/' | relative_url }}).

The best way to reach me is [email](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).
